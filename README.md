# Notely

**Turn your study notes into understanding, one question at a time.**

## Why Notely?

Notely was made to make studying from your own material more active. Instead of rereading notes, learners can test what they understand, see why an answer works, and ask follow-up questions while they study.

## Who is it for?

Notely is for students and independent learners who want a simple way to review class notes, readings, and other study material. It is designed for people who want practice and explanations without setting up a complicated study system.

## What can it do?

- Read PDF, DOCX, TXT, and Markdown files in the browser.
- Create multiple-choice quizzes from uploaded notes.
- Offer a choice between questions grounded in notes and broader topic exploration.
- Explain why the correct answer works and why a selected answer is wrong.
- Keep a quiz session with progress, results, and answer review.
- Provide **Ask Notely** for hints and explanations about the current question.
- Provide a separate **Notes chat** for open-ended questions about uploaded material.

## Main features

### Study from your notes
Choose a question count and difficulty, then generate a quiz based on the material you added.

### Learn from every answer
Get immediate feedback with reasoning for the correct answer and, when relevant, the mistaken choice.

### Ask questions while studying
Use **Ask Notely** for help with the current quiz question, or open **Notes chat** to explore the uploaded material in a separate conversation.

## Privacy

Files are read in your browser; the original files are not uploaded. Extracted text is sent to the Notely API and RootsSys when you generate quizzes or chat. Conversations and quiz progress are held in the current browser tab and are cleared when the page is refreshed.
